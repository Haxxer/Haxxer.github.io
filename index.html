Career Summary

I am a Technical Animator with over a decade of experience building tools, rigs, and animation pipelines for AAA games, from motion capture and character rigging through to engine export. I collaborate across art, animation, and engineering, working with props, vehicle, and character art teams, gameplay and cinematic animation teams, and rendering and core engine teams.

At Bohemia Interactive, I built animation tools and pipelines for DayZ’s 1.0 release, maintained the player animation state machine, and directed motion capture sessions. On Vigor, I led R&D on character implementation in engine.

At Rocksteady Studios, I rigged characters including Harley Quinn and Captain Boomerang for Suicide Squad: Kill The Justice League, and built tools connecting Maya, MotionBuilder, and Unreal, including a universal retargeting tool and a facial skinning decomposition pipeline I made 20x faster. As Lead Technical Animator, I managed and mentored the team and set the direction for our rigging and animation pipelines.
